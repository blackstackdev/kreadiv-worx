A little more room for life.

Quiet Calendar keeps work shifts, appointments and personal plans together on your Android phone. See your month, create rotating shifts and choose the reminders you need.

No account. No ads. Your calendar stays on your device until you choose to export or back it up.

What the free edition includes

  • Agenda and month views, with search
  • Work, personal and appointment events
  • Overnight, all-day and multi-day plans
  • Independent copies for shift rotations
  • Optional reminders and delivery settings
  • Calendar import/export and backup/restore

This free edition has no payment or expiry. A paid version is planned later; its features and price have not been announced.

Keeping your plans safe

Save a backup before changing phones or uninstalling. Backups are readable files, so keep them private. Reminder timing depends on your phone's notification, alarm and battery settings.
