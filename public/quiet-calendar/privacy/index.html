Quiet Calendar Privacy Policy
Effective date: 15 September 2026

Quiet Calendar is provided by Deen Storkey, operating as Krēˈādiv Worx. Contact: kreadivworx@proton.me.

Your calendar stays on your device
You enter event names, notes, dates, times, categories, time zones and optional reminders. Quiet Calendar stores these in its private on-device database to provide the calendar and reminders. We do not receive this information. There is no account, advertising, analytics, remote crash reporting or third-party SDK. This build has no internet permission and does not collect device identifiers, contacts, location, photos or your other calendars.

Files you choose
Import reads only a document you select through Android's document picker. Export and backup write to the destination you select. Files contain readable event data and are not encrypted by Quiet Calendar. A cloud file provider you choose may upload them under its own policy. A temporary export copy is held in private cache and removed when the export result returns; if interrupted it can remain until a later export, cache clearance or uninstall. The app requests no broad storage permission.

Notifications and background scheduling
If you enable reminders, event titles and start details are shown in Android notifications. The app requests notification permission on Android 13+ and optional precise alarm access on Android 12+. It receives device-restart and clock-change broadcasts to reschedule reminders. These permissions do not transmit data. Lock-screen notification settings are controlled by you and Android. You can disable reminders or permissions without losing saved events.

Home-screen agenda widget
If you add the optional widget, it displays upcoming event titles, dates, times and time zones on your home screen. People who can view your home screen may see these details. Remove the widget from your launcher to stop displaying them; removing it does not delete events. Refresh is periodic and after calendar changes, and can be delayed by battery settings. No information is sent to us.

Retention and deletion
Events remain until you delete them, clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall. Automatic operating-system backup is disabled. Delete any exported files separately in the location where you saved them. We cannot recover your calendar; keep a private backup if you need recovery or device transfer.

Support
If you choose to email us, your email provider sends your address and the message or attachments you choose to share. We use that correspondence to answer your request. Do not send calendar backups or sensitive notes unless necessary. You may request deletion of support correspondence by emailing the address above; records may be retained where legally required. We do not sell personal information.

Children and changes
The app has no social features, accounts or behavioral tracking and does not knowingly collect children's personal data. A future paid version or feature that changes data handling will require an updated policy. This policy describes the free edition only.
