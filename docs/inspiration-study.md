## Implement now

1. One unmistakable promise above the fold.
2. Real product evidence instead of decorative feature claims.
3. Honest status labels and a roadmap without invented dates.
4. One shared motion grammar built from paths, progress, and completion.

## Deliberately rejected

- Generic agency service menus: Krēˈādiv Worx currently has one active software shipping lane.
- Invented testimonials, customer counts, and sales claims: there is no verified basis yet.
- Constant motion, generic fade-up reveals, and disconnected 3D decoration: they would obscure the products and make the identity feel assembled rather than authored.
- Cape Town as an opening headline device: location is context, not the brand's primary promise.
- A generated social card: deferred until Raiden approves the live direction and final logo crop.

## Direction

An ink-led digital workshop: a dark, composed entrance opens into warm bone proof surfaces; cobalt carries momentum, coral signals human intervention, and one continuous-path language connects identity, progress, and finish. Typography stays confident and readable rather than stretched or performatively loud.
